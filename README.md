# EduFamily COMPASS – Student Career Planning & Family Decision Support

🌐 **Live Website:** aws-codeathon-2026.netlify.app

👥 **Team Name:** Cloudix

## 📌 About the Project

EduFamily COMPASS is a student-focused career planning and family decision-support platform designed to help students explore career opportunities, understand the skills required for their desired careers, and make informed decisions about their future.

The platform brings career pathways, skill roadmaps, personal priorities, and family discussions together in one organized digital workspace. It encourages students and their families to share perspectives, compare alternatives, understand concerns, and document decisions with clear reasoning.

EduFamily COMPASS aims to make career planning more structured, collaborative, and accessible, helping students approach their future with greater clarity and confidence.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often struggle to identify suitable career paths, understand the skills they need to develop, and decide which opportunities best align with their interests and goals. At the same time, differences between students' aspirations and family expectations can make career decisions more challenging.

EduFamily COMPASS addresses these challenges by providing a structured platform for career exploration, skill planning, option comparison, and family-supported decision-making.

## 💡 Our Solution

EduFamily COMPASS combines career planning and collaborative discussions in a single platform. Students can explore possible career directions, review learning roadmaps, and identify their priorities. The Family Workspace helps students and their families organize concerns, compare options, record tentative decisions, and document the reasons behind their choices.

The platform supports thoughtful decision-making while keeping students' goals and family perspectives part of the conversation.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Career Planning Dashboard
Provides a central interface for accessing the platform's career planning and decision-support sections in an organized way.

### 2. Career and Skills Exploration
Helps students explore career directions and understand the skills relevant to their professional interests and goals.

### 3. Career Pathways and Roadmaps
Presents structured career pathways and learning roadmaps to help students understand possible progression steps and plan their skill development.

### 4. Student Priorities and Concerns
Allows students to record their personal goals, preferences, priorities, and concerns related to educational and career decisions.

### 5. Family Priorities and Concerns
Provides a dedicated space for families to document their expectations, priorities, and concerns, encouraging better understanding between students and their families.

### 6. Family Discussion Workspace
Organizes career-related discussions through a discussion title, brief description, student priorities, family priorities, comparison options, tentative decisions, reasons, and discussion notes.

### 7. Option Comparison
Helps organize alternative career or educational choices so users can discuss and evaluate different possibilities before making a decision.

### 8. Tentative Decision and Reasoning
Enables users to record a possible decision along with the reasons supporting it, making the decision-making process easier to review.

### 9. Discussion Notes
Provides space to record important discussion points, concerns, and considerations for future reference.
